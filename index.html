why nothing

You reach for your phone because you don't know what else to do with a quiet moment.

blocking doesn't work

App blockers take the scroll away but leave an empty space. The restless mind looks for the next thing: another app, another feed, another tab. The habit just moves somewhere else.

give the mind something that doesn't stimulate it

What's missing isn't a wall. It's something to do that doesn't feed the loop: no new information, no reward, no next video. Just breath, a slow circle and a soft tone.

learn to sit blankly

Being able to sit with nothing is a skill, and like any skill it gets easier with practice. At first even a minute feels long. Over time the restlessness quiets down, and you'll notice it the next time you reach for your phone.

how it works

  1. When you notice yourself scrolling, open nothing.
  2. Pick ∞ or a length, then tap to begin.
  3. Drop your shoulders. Rest your attention between your eyebrows. Follow the circle: in, hold, out.
  4. Afterwards, note what came up if you like. Watch your sits grow longer.
